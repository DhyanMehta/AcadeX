# TS-12

## Early Academic Risk Detection & Student Intervention Platform

**Organization:** Dev IT Limited

---

## PROBLEM STATEMENT

In universities and colleges, academically at-risk students are often identified too late. Key indicators such as declining attendance, low internal marks, missed assignments, and irregular LMS activity are scattered across multiple systems and reviewed manually.

Faculty mentors rely on delayed reports or subjective judgment, resulting in late interventions such as counselling or remedial classes. By the time risk is identified, student performance and morale have already declined.

A unified system is needed to continuously analyze academic signals, detect early risk trends, and provide clear reasons for intervention—enabling proactive academic support and improved student outcomes.

---

## WINNING LOGIC

* **Multi-factor risk scoring:**
  The system must calculate student risk using multiple academic indicators such as attendance percentage, internal marks, and assignment completion status.

* **Explainable insights:**
  Each at-risk student must have clearly displayed top contributing reasons (e.g., low attendance, missing assignments) instead of just a score.

* **Actionable faculty dashboard:**
  Faculty should be able to filter and view at-risk students by class, subject, and severity level for quick decision-making.

* **Intervention recording:**
  Mentors must be able to log specific actions such as counselling sessions, remedial classes, or assignment extensions.

* **Performance improvement tracking:**
  The system should show comparison of student performance before and after intervention to measure effectiveness.

* **Timely alerts:**
  Automated alerts should notify mentors about high-risk students early (before major exams).

---

## USE CASES

### Student

* View personal academic risk score with reasons
* Receive improvement suggestions (e.g., increase attendance, complete pending assignments)
* Track progress over time after interventions

### Faculty Mentor

* Identify at-risk students from dashboard
* Analyze reasons behind risk
* Conduct counselling or academic support sessions
* Record intervention details
* Monitor student improvement

### Subject Teacher

* Upload internal assessment marks and assignment data
* Monitor subject-wise performance trends
* Identify students struggling in specific subjects

### Academic Coordinator

* View department/institution-level risk analytics
* Monitor effectiveness of interventions
* Identify academic performance patterns

---

## FUNCTIONALITIES TO BE ACHIEVED

1. Academic risk score generation per student
2. Explanation engine showing contributing academic factors
3. Faculty dashboard with filters by class, subject, and risk level
4. Student self-view portal with progress indicators
5. Intervention logging system (mentor actions + remarks)
6. Pre- and post-intervention performance comparison
7. Automated alerts for high-risk students
8. Downloadable academic risk summary reports (PDF/CSV)
